Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]  |  Last Updated: [DD/MM/YYYY]

Markzonic ("Company", "we", "our") provides digital services including Advertising, Marketing, SEO, Web & Android Development, Creative & Graphic Design, Video Editing and related solutions. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when refunds are permitted, the process for requesting refunds, and the protections we maintain to prevent fraudulent or abusive claims.

1. General Terms

  • All refund or cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to support@markzonic.com and include relevant supporting evidence.
  • Scope, timelines, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria and payment schedule will be defined in the proposal, invoice or agreement prior to project initiation.
  • This policy forms part of the contract between you and Markzonic; any deviation requires written agreement signed by an authorized representative of Markzonic.

2. Services Considered Non-Refundable

Due to the nature of the work and costs incurred, the following services are strictly non-refundable once initiated or delivered:

  • SEO Services: Ongoing, time-bound and performance-driven efforts.
  • Advertising Campaigns: Ad spend and associated service fees (ad spend is always non-refundable).
  • Web & App Development: After development begins or any code/design is delivered.
  • Creative Deliverables: Design concepts, graphics, videos, and any creative assets once initial concepts are shared.
  • Consultancy & Strategy Sessions: Any advisory session once conducted.
  • Third-Party Costs: Domain registrations, hosting, plugins, stock assets, licenses or other external third-party charges.

3. Refund Eligibility & Scope

  • Refunds are only eligible when:
    • The service has not been started;
    • The first agreed milestone is missed by Markzonic without any proven client-caused delay;
    • A deliverable is demonstrably incomplete or non-functional and Markzonic fails to remedy within the agreed remediation period.
  • Refunds, when approved, apply only to the service fee. All third-party costs or expenses paid on the client's behalf are non-refundable.
  • Any refunds approved will be reduced by actual expenses incurred and non-recoverable costs, including payment gateway fees.

4. Cancellation & Partial Refund Rules

  • Before Project Start: 100% refund of the service fee minus payment processing charges.
  • After Project Start but Before First Delivery: 50% refund of the service fee minus actual incurred expenses.
  • After First Delivery or any Partial Completion: No refund.
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted to support@markzonic.com. The effective cancellation date is the timestamp of our confirmation email acknowledging the cancellation.

5. Evidence, Logs & Dispute Resolution

We retain detailed records of our work — including time logs, version-controlled code commits, screenshots, design drafts, correspondence, analytics data and ad platform records — and will rely on those records to evaluate claims. If a dispute arises, both parties agree to provide reasonable cooperation and documentation.

6. Fraud & Abuse Prevention

  • False, misleading, or fraudulent refund claims will be rejected. We reserve the right to suspend services and pursue legal remedies for attempted fraud.
  • Clients found to make fraudulent claims may be blacklisted from future services and held responsible for collection costs, legal fees and other damages.
  • We may require identity verification, IP logs, device data, payment receipts or other proofs before processing a refund.

7. No Chargeback Without Prior Communication

By engaging our services, you agree not to initiate a chargeback or payment reversal without first contacting us and allowing a minimum of 14 business days to investigate and attempt resolution. Unauthorized chargebacks are considered a breach of contract and may result in legal action.

8. Processing Time & Method

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 7–14 business days using the original payment method when possible.
  • Processing timelines may vary depending on banking partners, payment gateways, and financial institutions; such external delays are outside Markzonic's control.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising under or in connection with this policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of [Your City, State].
