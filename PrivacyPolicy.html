Markzonic ("Company", "we", "our") provides Advertising, Marketing, SEO, Web & Android Development, Creative Design, Video Editing, Graphic Design and related digital services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal and business information submitted to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, with whom we share it, and your rights.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information collected:

  • Through our website at www.markzonic.com;
  • When you engage our services or submit project materials;
  • Via email, telephone, chat, third-party platforms and in-person interactions.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data under one or more of the following lawful bases (as applicable):

  • Performance of a contract (to deliver services you requested)
  • Legitimate interests (for security, fraud prevention, service improvement)
  • Consent (where required for marketing, cookies, or bespoke processing)
  • Legal obligation (taxation, legal compliance, dispute resolution)

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver, maintain and improve services and products;
  • To communicate project status, invoices, legal notices and support;
  • To run advertising campaigns and analyze performance (with client consent where required);
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse and to enforce our agreements;
  • To comply with legal obligations, subpoenas or court orders.

5. Data Sharing & Processors

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as necessary:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, hosting, analytics, email platforms — bound by written contracts including confidentiality and security clauses.
  • Ad & Analytics Platforms: For campaign execution and measurement. Data shared is limited to what is necessary for the service and covered by platform controls and policies.
  • Legal & Safety: To respond to lawful requests or to protect rights, property or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, sales, or asset transfers — data transferred under protections and notified to affected users where required.

6. Cross-Border Transfers

Data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries outside your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers (Standard Contractual Clauses, data processing agreements, or other safeguards) to provide an equivalent level of protection.

7. Retention

We retain personal and business data only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes or as required by law. Typical retention periods:

  • Project files & deliverables: 12 months after project closure (unless contract states otherwise)
  • Billing & tax records: 8 years
  • Support & legal records: 5 years or longer if needed for legal claims
  • Analytics data: 26 months or as per service configuration

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and tracking technologies for essential site functionality, analytics and advertising. Where required, we obtain consent for non-essential cookies. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser or via our cookie management tool where presented.

9. Security Measures

We apply technical and organizational measures to secure data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where applicable
  • Access controls, role-based permissions, and least-privilege principles
  • Regular backups and secure storage
  • Employee confidentiality agreements and periodic security training
  • Vulnerability scanning and incident response procedures

Note: While we implement robust security, no system is entirely immune to risk. We will notify affected parties and authorities as required by law in case of a data breach.

10. Children's Data

Our services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have obtained data from a minor, contact us and we will promptly delete such data.

11. Your Rights & Controls

Subject to applicable law, you may exercise the following rights by contacting us at privacy@markzonic.com and providing appropriate proof of identity:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion where there is no legal or contractual requirement to retain
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing

We may refuse requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive, or would adversely affect the rights of others. We will respond within statutory timelines (e.g., 30 days) or notify you if an extension is required.

12. Automated Decisions & Profiling

We may use automated tools for analytics and ad targeting. We do not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects without explicit consent and necessary safeguards. You can request human review where applicable.

13. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Where we process personal data on behalf of a client, we will enter into a Data Processing Agreement that sets out instructions, scope, duration, security measures, and subprocessors. Our DPA includes commitments aligned with GDPR and other international frameworks where relevant.

14. Third-Party Services & Links

Our site or services may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. When integrating third-party tools (e.g., analytics, ad platforms, payment gateways), those providers' privacy policies apply to their processing.

15. International & Specific Compliance

We aim to comply with applicable privacy laws including the Indian IT Act and rules, GDPR where applicable, and other regional laws like CCPA/CPRA for California residents. Compliance obligations will be addressed in agreements with clients and in-country requirements.

16. Complaints & Dispute Resolution

If you have a complaint about our privacy practices, contact privacy@markzonic.com. If unresolved, you may escalate to the relevant supervisory authority in your jurisdiction. For contractual disputes, parties will seek amicable resolution and may pursue legal remedies under the laws of India in [Your City, State].

17. Changes to This Policy
